# Snaps – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Snaps ("the extension") is a screenshot and image-editing extension for Google Chrome. Your privacy matters: **Snaps does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

## What Snaps does with your data
- **Screenshots** you capture are created on your device and stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) so you can reopen them in the gallery. They are never uploaded to any server.
- **Settings** (theme colour, default format, timer, after-capture behaviour) are stored locally using Chrome's `storage` API.
- Snaps captures only the tab you choose, only when you start a capture (button, keyboard shortcut or right-click menu).

## What Snaps does NOT do
- No accounts, sign-in or registration
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising
- No network requests to our servers or any third party – the extension works fully offline
- No remote code – all code is bundled in the extension package
- No reading of page content, browsing history or form data

## Permissions
| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| activeTab | Access the current tab only when you start a capture |
| scripting | Show the area-selection overlay and scroll the page for full-page capture |
| storage / unlimitedStorage | Save your settings and local screenshot history |
| downloads | Save screenshots to your Downloads folder when you ask |
| clipboardWrite | Copy a screenshot to the clipboard when you ask |
| contextMenus | Provide right-click capture shortcuts |

## Data retention and deletion
Everything is stored on your device. Delete individual screenshots or clear the whole history in the Snaps gallery, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension or clearing site data for the extension in Chrome.

## Children
Snaps does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
